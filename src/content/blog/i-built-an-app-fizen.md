<p>I was tired of maintaining and updating Excel sheets to track my investments, expenses, and income sources. So, out of pure frustration, I thought of building something simple that will migrate my personal finance tracking to a simple, intuitive, GUI based solution. Over the last few weekends, I vibe-coded and designed&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://fizen-personal-finance.vercel.app/"><strong>FiZEN</strong></a><strong> -</strong>&nbsp;a clean, intuitive personal finance tracker.</p><p>Honestly, I built this for my personal use. But then I thought maybe there is someone out there like me who loves simplicity, and probably they’ll appreciate it too. So, earlier this week I launched it on <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://peerlist.io/nehal/project/fizen"><strong>Peerlist Launchpad</strong></a>.</p><p>More about this app:</p><p>✨&nbsp;<strong>FiZEN - Your Path to Financial Peace of Mind</strong></p><p>A responsive web app where you can manually track your income, expenses, investments, loans, and overall financial health - all this without the spreadsheet fatigue. Built specifically for Indian working professionals and freelancers.</p><p>⚙️&nbsp;<strong>Key Features:</strong></p><ul><li><p>📊 Dashboard with snapshot of your finances along with budgets and saving goals</p></li><li><p>💰 Track multiple income sources like salary, rental income, freelance, or bonuses</p></li><li><p>📝 Add one-time and recurring expenses in seconds using Quick Action (Cmd K) menu</p></li><li><p>📈 Investment portfolio tracker (Mutual Funds, Stocks, FDs, PPF, NPS, etc.)</p></li><li><p>🏦 Loan and EMI tracker with payoff timelines</p></li><li><p>🔮 Detailed reports on spending, investments, and other important health metrics</p></li><li><p>🇮🇳&nbsp;Indian currency and financial year that starts in April</p></li></ul><p>🎨&nbsp;<strong>Why FiZEN?</strong></p><p>Because managing personal finance shouldn’t feel like work. The interface is clean, clutter-free, and mobile-friendly, so you can focus on your money, not on the tool.</p><p>Would love for you to check it out and share your feedback! 💙</p><p>👉&nbsp;Sign up and try it here:&nbsp;<a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://fizen-personal-finance.vercel.app">https://fizen-personal-finance.vercel.app</a>&nbsp;[<strong>It’s FREE!</strong>]</p><p>🔜&nbsp;<strong>Coming Soon in the Next Release</strong></p><ul><li><p>Taxes - manage and plan for income taxes</p></li><li><p>Statement upload - auto-add expenses from bank or credit card statements</p></li></ul><p>🛠️&nbsp;<strong>Tech Stack</strong></p><p>TypeScript, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript, PostgreSQL, Figma, Claude Sonnet 4, ChatGPT, Cursor, Copilot.</p><p>I would love to hear your comments and feedback.</p>
