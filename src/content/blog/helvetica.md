---
title: "Helvetica, anyone?"
description: "The downfall of Helvetica and rise of Inter, Geist, etc."
pubDate: 2025-05-08
---
<p>Back in the early 2000s when I got into design, Helvetica was the holy grail of typography. Clean, modern, every designer was in love with it.<br><br>Then Gotham arrived on the scene and suddenly every website and brand wanted that bold look.<br><br>The 2010s were all about open fonts. First Open Sans took over, then Google fonts like Roboto became the new default.<br><br>Now in the 2020s, we have Inter, Geist, etc. ruling the web.<br><br>Funny how no one talks about Helvetica anymore. I wonder if the new crop of UI designers even know its name.&nbsp;🙂</p>
