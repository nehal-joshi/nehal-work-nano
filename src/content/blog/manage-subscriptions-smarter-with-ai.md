(that "oh $hit" feeling when your card gets charged unexpectedly?) 😟💳💸</p><p>I’ve been there. And that’s why I built ✨ <strong>SubList</strong> ✨ an <strong>AI-powered subscription manager browser extension </strong>that automatically captures subscription details from billing/membership pages, and reminds you before you get billed.</p><p>✅ Tracks your monthly spend on digital services</p><p>✅ Sends timely renewal/cancellation reminders</p><p>✅ Works with Chrome, Edge, and Comet browsers</p><p>Now I can see all my subscriptions in one place along with the plan and renewal dates.
